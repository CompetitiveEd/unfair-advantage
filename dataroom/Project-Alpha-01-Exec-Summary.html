Situation

Apex Capital Partners acquired Project Alpha to build an integrated IT services platform through five acquisitions. The group now generates £185.2M revenue (declining from £191.4M) with approximately £9.1M EBITDA. The business holds Top 1% Global Vendor Partner status (5 consecutive years), 540 Cloud ERP clients, Tier-1 Security Expert accreditation, three owned data centres, and 1,800 mid-market clients. Exit governance is in place: a PE exit chairman and a serial PE exit CFO. Apex Capital is in year 5 of a typical 5-8 year hold. The PE structure carries £305M of external bank debt and £390M of cumulative preference shares accruing at £35M per year. Apex injected £10M of fresh equity recently.

Complication

The integration thesis has not executed. Five acquisitions remain separate legal entities. Cross-sell penetration across 1,800 clients stands at 0.17%. Operating margin has declined from 12.8% to 7.8% to 3.5%. Gross margin collapsed 740 basis points in a single year. Group revenue is declining. Cash interest coverage is 0.31x (£9.1M EBITDA against £28.5M cash interest). The bank facility has been amended. Going concern is stated for twelve months only. The entire operating leadership has departed within twelve months. There is no acting CEO. The public employee rating of 2.4/5 is the lowest in the peer set. This pattern -- adjacency acquisitions without integration capability, cross-sell not enabled from week one, no dedicated integration team -- is documented across hundreds of PE buy-and-build failures. The assets are exceptional. The financial structure around them is under severe pressure.

Answer

The business cannot go to market in this condition. Any sophisticated buyer will review the statutory filings and know the cash interest coverage is 0.31x. They will use that leverage. Apex Capital needs 6-12 months of operational improvement before engaging buyers. Three exit stories remain credible, but only if margin recovers to 8%+ and cross-sell is demonstrable.

A 90-day programme structured around three commercial priorities: defend (retain the top 50 accounts, protect renewals against NexusTech), expand (cross-sell across the 540 Cloud ERP clients using cross-entity pod teams with shared targets and shared comp), and grow (launch the legacy ERP end-of-life migration campaign to convert guaranteed demand into revenue). The programme provides external commercial leadership -- intelligence gathering, stakeholder profiling, competitive analysis, cross-sell architecture, and pursuit management -- filling the CEO-shaped gap with AI-accelerated execution rather than another executive hire. Every month of delay costs £2.9M in preference share accrual and deepens the negotiating disadvantage.
