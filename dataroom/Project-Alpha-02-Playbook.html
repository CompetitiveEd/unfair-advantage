Tower detail

T1: People & Performance
No CEO. Vacancy since last quarter. Replacement lasted 4 months (DNA mismatch). No search underway. COO running operations. Revenue number is orphaned. Fix: GAIN fills the coordination gap. CEO search is a board decision (Step 23 context).
5 senior departures in 12 months. Including Founder, CEO, CCO, Group MD, and GC. Plus acquired founders left sector. Fix: Retention audit (Step 3). Pulse survey (Step 6). Cannot replace what left -- protect what remains.
Employee Sentiment 2.4/5. Lowest in peer set (Vector 3.8, CloudCore 3.9, Nova 4.2). 26% recommend. Compensation 2.6/5. Below-inflation pay 4 consecutive years. Fix: Address top 3 complaints (Step 6). Budget: allocate funds to close gaps for top 20% of roles.
HR Leader has multiple hats. Acting as Board Advisor + Exec Coach + Change Specialist. Role clarity needed. Fix: GAIN handles change management, freeing HR for people focus.
T2: Integration & Proposition
20 active legal entities. 27 companies at same address. 5 acquisitions still separate. Governance burden extraordinary. Fix: Entity consolidation plan (Step 18). Target: reduce to 5-7 entities.
Cross-sell 0.17%. 3 named examples across 1,800 clients. The acquisition thesis required cross-sell. After 4 years it has not happened. Fix: Map 540 clients first (Step 5). Launch 10 pursuits (Step 13). Cross-sell comp plan (Step 12).
Zero security case studies. Tier-1 Security Expert with no customer evidence. Capability credential without proof. Fix: 1 security case study by day 90 (Step 22). Or downgrade the marketing claim.
Subsidiary websites stale. Hundreds of case studies pre-2021. None reference the Group entity. Fix: Website refresh (Step 14). Retire stale subsidiary pages. Unified evidence library.
No unified battle card. Sellers cannot describe Group as one company. Fix: Battle card v1 (Step 11). GAIN writes, CMO reviews, 5 sellers test.
T3: Revenue Engine
No cross-sell comp plan. 0.17% cross-sell strongly suggests zero incentive to sell across entities. Fix: Design and launch cross-sell comp (Step 12). Cost: manageable. Impact: unlocks the PE thesis.
1 GTM hire of 24 open roles. Delivery-heavy hiring when margin is compressing. Inconsistent with exit preparation. Fix: Rebalance ratio 1:3 GTM to delivery (Step 10). Post cross-sell and security sales roles.
Pipeline visibility DARK. No unified pipeline view across entities. Cannot manage what you cannot see. Fix: Unified pipeline report (Step 19). Even a spreadsheet is better than nothing.
540 Legacy ERP clients must migrate. Legacy end-of-life forces Cloud migration. Guaranteed implementation revenue. Fix: Automated campaign (Step 20). GAIN segments, messages, launches.
T4: Exit Readiness
Margin 12.8% to 7.8% and declining. Each £1 of new revenue costs more than £1 to deliver. RPE £136K vs peers £210-270K. Fix: Steps 1-2 confirm the number. Steps 5, 12, 13 drive cross-sell revenue. Target: 10%+ margin by exit.
RPE £136K (peers: Vector £210K, CloudCore £270K). Headcount growing faster than revenue. Fix: Entity consolidation removes duplicated roles. Cross-sell grows revenue without proportional headcount.
Buyer story incomplete. Revenue growing but margin falling. Integration incomplete. No CEO. Buyers see risk. Fix: Steps 21-25 build the story. "Margin recovering, cross-sell proven, data room ready."
Governance hires complete. Chairman (exit-grade) and CFO (serial PE exit) both in place. Professional governance. Already done. Buyer confidence is there at board level.
