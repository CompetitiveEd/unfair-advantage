## PHASE 4: FINANCIAL LEAK EXTRACTION

Search for waste across these 5 domains. For EACH leak found, output:

| THE LEAK | THE PROCESS (Named) | THE METRIC | THE CALCULATION | THE COST (£) | 3-YR TREND | THE OWNER (Job Title) | CONFIDENCE | GAIN DRIVER |

**3-YR TREND column (NEW — v5):** Is this leak growing, stable, or shrinking? A leak that's been £400K/yr for 3 years is structural. A leak that grew from £200K to £400K is accelerating. The trend changes the urgency of the conversation.

The GAIN DRIVER column maps each leak to the buyer psychology it triggers:
— **Safety:** Leak creates regulatory, reputational, or operational risk if unfixed
— **Order:** Leak wastes time, creates complexity, or adds cognitive load
— **Achievement:** Fixing the leak creates a visible, measurable win for the owner

Domains:
1. **Manual Processes** — repetitive human tasks that software could eliminate
2. **Integration Friction** — data moving between systems via spreadsheets or manual re-entry
3. **Compliance Burden** — regulatory overhead that could be automated or streamlined
4. **Vendor Dependency** — over-reliance on a single vendor creating switching-cost traps
5. **Technical Debt** — legacy systems draining maintenance budget that could fund transformation

**MECE SELF-CHECK:** Confirm these 5 categories do not overlap and collectively cover the target's cost landscape. If a leak doesn't fit these 5, create a 6th category and flag it.

## PHASE 5: SOURCE TRIANGULATION (Bellingcat)

For every leak with a cost estimate above £500K/year:
1. **SOURCE 1:** Where does this number come from? (G-01 data, annual report, industry benchmark — name it)
2. **SOURCE 2:** Can it be corroborated from an independent source? (Second data point, different methodology, peer comparison from Phase 2)
3. **TRIANGULATION VERDICT:** CONFIRMED (2+ sources agree within 20%), INDICATIVE (sources directionally aligned but >20% variance), or UNVERIFIED (single source only)

| LEAK | SOURCE 1 | SOURCE 2 | VARIANCE | VERDICT |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Any leak marked UNVERIFIED must have its confidence downgraded to LOW in Phase 4 table. Flag it for operator attention.

Check for corporate distress signals that affect financial reliability:

**OUTPUT:** If 2+ red flags detected → **DISTRESS WARNING.** This changes the entire financial narrative: the target may not be able to fund the intervention, or may be under pressure that makes the cost argument MORE urgent (safety driver). Cross-reference with G-02B if available.

## PHASE 9: SERVICE-PROFIT CHAIN COST MAPPING (Heskett)

For the top 3 leaks, trace the cascade to revenue impact:
1. **THE OPERATIONAL FAILURE:** What breaks or degrades? (Name the process)
2. **THE SERVICE IMPACT:** How does this affect the customer/end-user experience?
3. **THE REVENUE CONSEQUENCE:** Does this cause churn, reduce NPS, trigger penalties, or create regulatory exposure?
4. **THE MULTIPLIER:** What is the ratio of operational cost to revenue impact? (e.g., "£1 of downtime cost creates £4.2 of customer impact")
5. **THE 3-YEAR VIEW (NEW):** Is this multiplier growing? A service failure that cost 3x in Year 1 but 5x in Year 3 shows a deteriorating service-profit chain.

This transforms "we save you X" into "inaction costs you X in waste PLUS Y in revenue risk."
