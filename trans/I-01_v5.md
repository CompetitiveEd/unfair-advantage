## PHASE 1: THE FAILURE SCENARIOS

Generate 5-7 distinct scenarios. For EACH:

| SCENARIO | PROBABILITY | TRIGGER | CASCADE PATH | CRITERION IMPACT | EARLY WARNING | DEFENCE |

1. **THE POLITICAL KILL:** Which role vetoed us? Kegan immunity drove the veto. If G-03 available, show network propagation.
2. **THE COMPETITIVE DISPLACEMENT:** How did a competitor outmanoeuvre us? What did they say to whom? Which verification question did we fail to plant?
3. **THE NO-DECISION DEATH:** Champion lost momentum. Internal politics consumed oxygen. What specific obstacle did they face?
4. **THE PRICE COLLAPSE:** Won on capability, lost on price. What happened in commercial negotiation?
5. **THE EXECUTION DOUBT:** They liked us but didn't believe we could deliver at scale. What evidence were we missing?
6. **THE THEME COLLAPSE:** A win theme we rated "fortress" turned out weaker. Which theme, what exposed it?
7. **THE CHAMPION ISOLATION:** Champion neutralised politically. Who isolated them, how?
8. **THE COMP-DRIVEN INERTIA (v5):** Decision-maker's bonus doesn't reward fixing this problem. They optimise for their bonus metrics, not for what the company needs. If G-03 v5 comp data available: does the sponsor's compensation work FOR or AGAINST this deal?
9. **THE VENDOR LOYALTY OVERRIDE (v5):** A key evaluator has a personal relationship with a competitor from their previous company. The evaluation was never genuinely competitive. If G-03 v5 vendor loyalty data available: is this scenario real?
10. **THE JELLY STALL (v5):** The project this deal depends on was already stalled (Jelly) before we engaged. The initiative is announced but not executing. Risk isn't losing to a competitor — it's that nothing happens. If G-01 v5 BPF available: check Jelly status.
