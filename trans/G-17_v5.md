# G-17: SOURCE ASSEMBLY PROTOCOL v5 — Data Assembly Checklist
## GAIN Protocol — Gold Standard v5
## 30-Source OSINT Checklist + 13-Pillar Source Map + Bellingcat Grading + Human Intelligence Injection

---

## WHAT THIS IS

This is NOT a generative prompt. This is an OPERATIONAL CHECKLIST for the human operator. No AI output required — the operator uses this to gather source material before running the GAIN chain.

**THE RULE:** Garbage in = garbage out. Every minute spent gathering high-quality sources saves 10 minutes of AI hallucination correction. This checklist ensures you feed the GAIN chain with the right data.

**v5 UPGRADE:** Expanded from 17 sources to 30 (aligned with G-22's 5-tier OSINT model). Source requirements mapped to G-01 v5's 13 pillars. Bellingcat grading tells you which sources to trust most.

### TIER E — HUMAN INTELLIGENCE (The AI Can't Get This — YOU Must Inject It)
*This is what separates a good brief from a great one. No estimated time — this is accumulated over the relationship.*

| # | SOURCE | WHAT TO CAPTURE | FORMAT FOR AI INJECTION |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | **Corridor Conversations** | What was said informally, off the record | [HUMINT] [Date] [Context]: "[Quote or paraphrase]" |
| H2 | **Previous Meeting Notes** | Key points, commitments, body language observations | [MEETING] [Date] [Attendees]: [Summary] |
| H3 | **CRM History** | Activity log — dates of contact, who initiated, outcomes | [CRM] [Date range]: [Activity summary] |
| H4 | **Event / Site Visit Observations** | Physical environment, technology visible, culture signals | [VISUAL] [Date] [Location]: [Observations] |
| H5 | **Competitor Sightings** | Competitor presence, messaging, relationships observed | [COMPETITIVE] [Date]: [What was seen/heard] |
| H6 | **Internal Knowledge** | What your organisation already knows about this company | [INTERNAL] [Source]: [Intelligence] |
| H7 | **Photo Evidence** | Office photos, reception, events, technology visible | Upload directly to G-01 (Visual Evidence variable) |

**CONTEXT INJECTION FORMAT:** When pasting human intelligence into AI prompts, use the tags above so the AI can parse it correctly and mark it as HUMINT (distinct from public sources).

## QUICK-START: MINIMUM VIABLE SOURCE PACK

If you only have 15 minutes, gather these 5:

1. **Annual Report** (last year minimum, last 3 years ideal) — #1
2. **Company Website** (strategy + leadership pages) — #9, #11
3. **LinkedIn Profile** of key stakeholder — #25
4. **Glassdoor** (overall rating + 3yr trend) — #17
5. **Careers Page** (10 JDs from the relevant department) — #12

This gives G-01, G-03/G-09, and G-10 enough to produce useful output. Everything else enriches and triangulates.

## SOURCE QUALITY GATE (Gawande)

Before running ANY GAIN prompt, verify:

- [ ] At least 1 Tier A source gathered (legal/financial)
- [ ] At least 2 Tier B sources gathered (corporate comms)
- [ ] At least 1 Tier C source gathered (independent)
- [ ] For people prompts (G-03/G-09): LinkedIn profile gathered (#25)
- [ ] For financial prompts (G-02/G-02B): Annual Report gathered (#1)
- [ ] Human intelligence injected using correct tags (Tier E)
- [ ] Sources span at least 2 tiers (prevents single-source bias)
- [ ] 3-year data gathered where available (not just current year)

**IF SOURCE QUALITY GATE FAILS:** Flag to operator: "Insufficient sources. Output confidence will be LOW. Gather [specific missing sources] before proceeding, or accept preliminary output."
