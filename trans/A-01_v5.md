## PHASE 5B: KOTTER URGENCY ASSESSMENT (v4 NEW)

Assess the organisational urgency behind this RFP using Kotter's 8-Step framework:

| URGENCY SIGNAL | EVIDENCE FROM RFP/INTERACTION DATA | STRENGTH |
|---|---|---|
| **Burning Platform:** Is there an existential threat driving this? | | HIGH/MED/LOW |
| **Guiding Coalition:** Is there a senior leadership group visibly sponsoring this? | | |
| **Vision Clarity:** Does the RFP articulate a clear future state, or is it reactive? | | |
| **Quick Wins Demanded:** Does the RFP emphasise early milestones / phased delivery? | | |
| **Anchoring in Culture:** Does the RFP reference embedding change, not just implementing technology? | | |

**URGENCY VERDICT:**
— **HIGH URGENCY (3+ strong signals):** This is a real transformation programme. Decision timeline is firm. Lead with execution capability and speed.
— **MEDIUM URGENCY (1-2 strong signals):** Mixed signals. Some urgency but may stall at Board approval. Probe for the real deadline.
— **LOW URGENCY (0 strong signals):** This may be exploratory or a benchmarking exercise. Risk of no-decision. Lead with cost-of-inaction to CREATE urgency.

**STRATEGIC IMPLICATION:** Kotter urgency level determines your ENTIRE response tone. High urgency = match their speed, show you can execute NOW. Low urgency = your job is to build the burning platform they haven't built internally.

## PHASE 6B: RFP REALITY CHECK (v5 — Execute if G-22 or G-01 BPF available)

Cross-reference every major RFP claim against what G-01/G-22 intelligence actually shows:

| RFP CLAIM | G-01/G-22 EVIDENCE | ALIGNED? | IMPLICATION |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Digital transformation programme" | G-22 shows H1 cost-cutting, no transformation budget | NO | RFP language is aspirational, not funded. Expect descoping. |
| "Innovation-led approach required" | G-01 Pillar 13: CEO bonus on EPS (margin) | NO | They SAY innovation but PAY for cost-cutting. Lead with efficiency. |
| "Strategic partnership model" | G-10 hiring signals show commodity JD language | PARTIAL | They want partnership but are staffing for commodity management. |

**THE REALITY CHECK RULE:** Where the RFP narrative contradicts the intelligence, the intelligence wins. The RFP was written by committee; the financials were written by auditors. Auditors are more honest.

**BPF ALIGNMENT:** If G-01 BPF Seed is available, check: does this RFP map to a specific BPF Goal/Initiative? If YES → the project has strategic support. If NO → it may be orphaned from the corporate strategy (higher stall risk).

**JELLY CHECK:** If the BPF initiative this RFP maps to is flagged as Jelly (announced but stalled), the RFP may be an attempt to restart — or it may be procurement theatre. Probe with: "How long has this programme been in planning?"

## PHASE 6C: BUDGET SANITY CHECK (v5 — Execute if G-02 v5 available)

Does the stated RFP scope match the target's financial capacity?

| SANITY CHECK | EVIDENCE | VERDICT |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope vs Revenue:** Is the contract value proportionate to the company's revenue? | [G-02 revenue data] | Proportionate / Ambitious / Unrealistic |
| **Scope vs IT Spend:** Does the scope fit within typical IT spend % for this sector? | [G-02 Pillar 5 / peer benchmarking] | Within norms / Above norms |
| **Scope vs Credit:** Can they fund this given their credit trajectory? | [G-02 Phase 3 / G-02B] | Strong / Strained / At Risk |
| **Scope vs Free Cash Flow:** Is FCF sufficient to fund the transition year? | [G-02 Phase 1] | Yes / Tight / No |

**IF BUDGET SANITY FAILS:** Flag immediately: "The RFP scope exceeds what the financial evidence supports. Expect one of: (1) Descoping during negotiation, (2) Phased approach forced by CFO, (3) Programme deferred post-evaluation. Adjust bid investment accordingly."

## PHASE 6D: SPONSOR INCENTIVE CHECK (v5 — Execute if G-03 v5 comp data available)

Does the RFP sponsor's personal compensation align with what they're buying?

| SPONSOR | THEIR BONUS METRIC | RFP OBJECTIVE | ALIGNED? | RISK |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Name/Role from G-03] | [What their bonus measures] | [What the RFP says they're buying] | Y/N | [If NO: the deal stalls because the sponsor isn't personally rewarded for success] |

**THE INCENTIVE RULE:** If the CIO's bonus is on cost reduction and the RFP is about innovation, there's a structural misalignment. The sponsor will champion it publicly but won't fight for it when budget pressure hits — because their bonus doesn't reward it.

**IF MISALIGNMENT DETECTED:**
- Reframe your response to show how the project impacts THEIR bonus metric, not just the RFP's stated objective
- Identify a DIFFERENT sponsor whose comp IS aligned — they're the real champion
- Flag in I-01 (Pre-Mortem) as a Comp-Driven Inertia failure scenario

**IF COMP DATA UNAVAILABLE:** Flag: "Sponsor incentive check requires G-03 v5 with compensation data. Without it, assume alignment and verify in meetings."
