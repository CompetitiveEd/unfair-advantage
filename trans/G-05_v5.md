## PHASE 4B: JELLY QUALIFICATION QUESTION (NEW — v5)

(Execute if G-01 v5 BPF Seed OR G-22 Waterfall is available. This is the senior-level conversation that positions you as a peer, not a seller.)

**THE TEMPLATE:**
"Your annual report outlines [strategy from BPF/G-22]. Your KPIs suggest [metrics] should be moving. [Initiative A] appears to be progressing but [Initiative B] seems stalled. Is that a fair read?"

**WHY THIS WORKS:** This is diagnosis, not selling. You're demonstrating that you've done your homework, you understand their business at a strategic level, and you're asking them to confirm or correct — which gives them the opportunity to share what's really happening.

**BUILD THE SPECIFIC QUESTION:**

| ELEMENT | SOURCE | THIS MEETING'S VERSION |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy reference | G-01 Pillar 1 or G-22 Section 1 | "[Specific strategy from their annual report]" |
| KPI reference | G-01 Pillar 2 or G-02 P&L Waterfall | "[Specific metric that should be moving]" |
| Progressing initiative | G-01 BPF (status: Executing) | "[Initiative with visible progress]" |
| Stalled initiative | G-01 BPF (status: Jelly/Stalled) | "[Initiative announced but not progressing]" |

**KÜBLER-ROSS GATE:**
- Denial/Anger → DO NOT use the full Jelly Question. Too direct. Instead: "I noticed [initiative] was mentioned in the annual report. How's that tracking?"
- Bargaining → Use the full question but soften: "I may have this wrong, but it looks like..."
- Acceptance → Full question, direct delivery.

**IF BPF/G-22 NOT AVAILABLE:** Skip this phase. Flag: "Jelly Qualification Question requires G-01 v5 BPF Seed or G-22 Waterfall data. Without strategic context, use standard discovery approach."

## PHASE 4D: DIALOGUE PLANNING — EMOTIONAL SHIFT METHODOLOGY (NEW — v5)

(From DSE — Driving Strategic Engagement. Structures the entire meeting as an emotional journey.)

**THE MEETING ARC:**

| PHASE | EMOTIONAL STATE | YOUR ACTION | OUTCOME |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Opening (0-2 min) | Polite but guarded | Rapport Hook + Permission Play (Camp) | They relax |
| 2. The Hook (2-5 min) | Curious | BPF Discovery or Jelly Question | They lean forward |
| 3. The Shift (5-10 min) | Concerned or excited | Bleeding Neck evidence + Peer comparison | They feel the urgency |
| 4. The Pivot (10-13 min) | Engaged | Connect their concern to what you do (obliquely) | They see the path |
| 5. The Close (13-15 min) | Committed | Next step commitment | They agree to action |

**THE WASHROOM CONVERSATION:** After the meeting, what does this person say to their colleague on the way back to their desk? Script the sentence you WANT them to say:

"[Name] just told me something about [topic] that I hadn't thought about. We should probably look at this."

**If that sentence doesn't work as the output of this meeting, the script needs rewriting.**

**THE HOOK:** What is the single most surprising or compelling thing you can say in this meeting that they haven't heard from anyone else? This must come from G-01/G-22/G-02 intelligence — not from your product knowledge. The hook is about THEM, not you.

## PHASE 5: THE 4-PART SCRIPT (All Paths)

### PART 1: THE AUTHENTIC OPENER

Use G-03 Rapport Hook. Align VAK. Adapt Kegan stage.

**MODALITY ADAPTATION:**
* Visual → Reference something visible (report, dashboard)
* Auditory → Reference something they said (quote, position)
* Kinesthetic → Reference something experiential (project, site)

**LANDMINE CHECK:** Review G-03 "What NOT to Do." Nothing triggers a landmine.

**GAIN DRIVER INTEGRATION:**

| GAIN DRIVER | OPENER ORIENTATION |
|---|---|
| Safety | Reassurance, risk acknowledgment |
| Order | Structure, agenda setting |
| Power | Deference to authority |
| Achievement | Recognition of their work |

### PART 2: THE CREDIBILITY BRIDGE

Ghost Connections, shared history, or case study. Under 30 seconds. Low self-orientation (Maister).

If I-02 themes available, weave the most relevant — as understanding, not pitch.

### PART 3: THE DISRUPTION QUESTION

**OPTION A — Narrative Gap (from G-04):** Adapted to Kegan stage:
* Stage 3 → "I've heard others say..."
* Stage 4 → "I'd challenge the assumption that..."

**OPTION B — Jelly Question (v5, from Phase 4B):** If BPF data available AND meeting is with senior executive, use the Jelly Qualification Question instead. It's more powerful because it's specific to THEIR strategy, not a generic industry challenge.

**SELECTION RULE:** Use Jelly Question for C-suite/VP (they own strategy). Use Narrative Gap for operational/mid-level (they own execution).

**KÜBLER-ROSS GATE:** If Denial/Anger → replace with softer: "I'm curious how you're thinking about [topic]."

**PERMISSION PLAY (Camp):** Give permission to disagree before asking.

If I-03 available, position to pre-empt competitor pitch.

### PART 4: THE PIVOT

Guide from answer to Bleeding Neck financial leak. Don't pitch. Let data speak.

If they own a G-02 leak, reference obliquely and let them confirm.
