The Multiple Changes Even If Revenue Doesn't
Boutique technology firm. £9M revenue. Every deal won by one person. The business was unsellable — not because of revenue, but because it walked out the door every night. The valuation was the founder. Remove the founder dependency, the multiple changes overnight.
3-year exit plan activated. Business self-sustaining. Sales team doubled. 75 of 76 target events attended. Founder making judgment calls with time to think instead of closing every deal himself.

The Diagnosis

No capability infrastructure. No account intelligence. No whitespace mapping. Sellers maintaining, not growing. Motivation driven by fear, not purpose. The business had revenue but no system.

The Fix

Structural, not training. New sales leadership. Restructured finance for decision-quality data. GAIN methodology embedded. Pivot from founder-led to customer intimacy model.

The PE Lens

Key-person risk eliminated. Repeatable revenue model in place. Sales team operates independently. Exit multiple improves on structure alone — before revenue growth even compounds.

Where This Breaks
The Middle Management Gap

The methodology relies on aligning with C-Suite financial mandates. If your team is too terrified to bypass the VP of IT or the Procurement Lead, the strategy will fail. Middle managers buy features; boards buy risk mitigation.

The Confidence Problem

You can give a team the perfect AI-generated executive dossier. But if they lack the presence to walk into a boardroom and challenge a CFO's worldview, the data is useless. Tools do not equal talent.
