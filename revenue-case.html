Where This Fails
Wrong Deal Selected

Trying to rescue a mathematically dead deal. If the competitor's software is physically integrated, strategy won't save you.

No Sponsor Authority

Deploying at mid-management level. GAIN requires C-Suite engagement. Middle management rejects risk-based narratives.

Success Isn't Defined

If the team can't articulate what a good quarter looks like beyond a revenue number, the methodology has nothing to anchor to.
